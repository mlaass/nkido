Mitigation: interleave baseline re-measurement every iteration\n   296\t   (speedup is always ratio-to-a-fresh-baseline, never to a number from an hour\n   297\t   ago); mark runs `unstable` when the fresh baseline drifts > 10 % from the\n   298\t   pinned one.\n   299\t2. **Model edits the benchmark or the tests.** Rejected by the allowlist (\u00a75.5).\n   300\t3. **Patch does not compile.** Counts as an iteration; compiler output is fed\n   301\t   back as the rejection reason.\n   302\t4. **Bit-identity impossible** (`tanh`, `tan`, `pow`, FP reassociation).
