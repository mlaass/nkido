Same sound, up to 31× faster

AI agents rewrote two of nkido's audio effects in AVX2. These rewrites don't produce bit-identical output, so every example here is rendered twice, with the original code and with the agent's code. Each example has a play button for the original render, one for the agent's render, and one for the difference.

Difference plays the after-minus-before signal boosted by 100 dB. At normal level it peaks at -115 dBFS or lower, below the 16-bit noise floor. Turn your volume down before you play it.

Drum loops: Amen break, acoustic old-school drum loop and rock drum loop from Orange Free Sounds, licensed CC BY 4.0. Basslines from nkido's welcome patches (acid 303 and rock groove).

Rendered offline at 48 kHz with nkido render --float32. Audio is lossless FLAC. The spectra are Welch power spectra of the mono sum.
